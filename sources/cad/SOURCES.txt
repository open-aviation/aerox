aerox manufacturer CAD source collection
========================================

Last checked and downloaded: 2026-08-06

Purpose
-------
This directory stores the original manufacturer ZIP archives and their
extracted CAD drawings used to research wing, tail, control-surface, and
overall-aircraft geometry for the bundled aerox aircraft records.

Directory layout
----------------
airbus/archives/   Original Airbus ZIP downloads (not in Git; see the landing pages)
airbus/drawings/   Extracted Airbus DWG files, grouped by archive name
boeing/archives/   Original Boeing ZIP downloads (not in Git; see the landing pages)
boeing/drawings/   Extracted Boeing DXF/DWG files, grouped by archive name
converted/         DXF conversions of the DWG drawings

The collection contains 50 archives and 50 extracted CAD files:

  Airbus: 18 archives covering 17 bundled aircraft records
  Boeing: 32 archives covering 25 bundled aircraft records

The archive count exceeds the aircraft count because configuration-specific
alternatives are intentionally preserved. Examples include baseline/winglet
737 drawings, passenger/freighter 747-8 drawings, and both A330-200 vertical
tail configurations. A source drawing must not be applied to a YAML record
until its model and modification applicability have been resolved.

Source status and limitations
-----------------------------
Airbus landing page:
https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/en/customer-care/fleet-wide-care/airport-operations-and-aircraft-characteristics/autocad-3-view-aircraft-drawings

Airbus states that these three-view drawings are for airport-planning purposes
and accurate to within 10 centimetres.

Boeing landing page:
https://www.boeing.com/commercial/airports/3-view

Boeing states that these plan, side, and front views are for airport-planning
purposes and should be accurate within plus or minus 6 inches. Boeing also
instructs users to reconcile them with the dimensions in the applicable
Airplane Characteristics for Airport Planning document.

These are manufacturer-published planning drawings, not type-design drawings
and not certification findings. A dimension explicitly printed in a drawing
may be recorded as manufacturer-published-planning. Geometry measured from a
CAD entity must be recorded as inferred-with-uncertainty. Values calculated
from measured planform inputs must retain the input measurements, formula, and
uncertainty. Do not label drawing-derived values as certified.

The manufacturers' copyright and reuse terms apply to the archives and
drawings in this directory.

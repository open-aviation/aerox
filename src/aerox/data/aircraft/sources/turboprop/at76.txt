CERTIFICATION SCOPE
  Model or family: ATR 72-212A with MOD 5948 (NAS), commercial designation ATR 72-600
  Authority: EASA
  Type certificate: EASA.A.084
  Applicability: EASA.A.084 Issue 14 notes that "ATR 72-600" is the commercial designation of the ATR 72-212A fitted with the New Avionic Suite (MOD 5948); it is not a separate model or variant. The airframe geometry is shared with the ATR 72-500 (also ATR 72-212A). The record uses the highest certified weight variant (MOD 6219 / WV50). The -600F cargo conversion (MOD 7900) is excluded.

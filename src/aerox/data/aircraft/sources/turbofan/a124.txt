CERTIFICATION SCOPE
  Model or family: An-124-100 (civil version of the An-124)
  Authority: Interstate Aviation Committee Aviation Register (IAC AR)
  Type certificate: not retrieved; the type certificate was issued in 1992 according to the operator booklet
  Applicability: An-124-100 at 392 t MTOW with D-18T series 3 engines. The military An-124 (405 t), the An-124-100M, the An-124-100-150 and the An-124-111VD upgrades (402 t MTOW) are excluded. No type-certificate data sheet for the An-124-100 was found in public; the geometry and limits come from a 2005 civil-aviation flight-school textbook of the An-124-100 and from operator publications.

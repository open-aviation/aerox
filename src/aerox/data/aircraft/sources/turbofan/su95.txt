CERTIFICATION SCOPE
  Model or family: RRJ-95B
  Authority: Rosaviatsia validated by EASA
  Type certificate: EASA.IM.A.176; FATA-01020A
  Applicability: SSJ100/95B is the commercial name of the RRJ-95B model. The SSJ100/95LR (RRJ-95LR-100, SaM146-1S18, EASA major change 10060566) shares the airframe and dimensions but has a 49,450 kg MTOW; the record uses RRJ-95B values unless a field states otherwise. The SJ-100 import-substituted variant (PD-8 engines, new winglets) is excluded. EASA suspended this type certificate in March 2022; the Issue 04 data sheet remains the latest published certified data.
